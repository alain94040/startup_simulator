Why the arc works: The cut was never a bad decision — shipping lean is right. The arc teaches that users tell you what you missed, and the window to act cheaply is narrow: after beta, before launch. Post-launch fixes cost 50% more and fight existing user expectations. Dropping every hint is a passive choice to not pivot — the bad_retention card makes that passive choice visible and consequential.
